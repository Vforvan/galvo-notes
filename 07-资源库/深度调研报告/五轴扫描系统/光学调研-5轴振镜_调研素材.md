# 工业 5 轴振镜：轴配置与整条光路 — 事实核查调研

> 核查日期：本轮调研。标注规则：✅ 一手来源（厂商官网/数据手册/专利原文/控制卡手册）｜🟡 二手转述｜❌ 未找到公开来源

---

## 0. 核心结论（先说最重要的）

**「5 轴振镜」在业界不是一个标准化术语。** 我找到至少 5 种互不兼容的用法，且**没有**任何一家厂商把它定义为「X/Y/Z/U/V 五个振镜电机」。用户当前的理解（"只用 5 个电机实现的结构"）只在其中一个分支上成立，必须修正。

## 1. 「5 轴」到底指哪 5 个轴

### 1.1 RAYLASE 的官方定义（最接近用户设想）✅

RAYLASE SP-ICE 3 控制卡手册在 Scan Head Format 表中把 5 个轴定义为 **X、Y、Z、ZoomZ（光学变焦）、Extra Z / Auxiliary（辅助 Z）**：

| 轴 | 手册原文功能 |
|---|---|
| X, Y | 双振镜偏转 |
| Z | "controls the laser focus within the scanner's field volume" |
| **ZoomZ** | "**optically** changes the laser's spot diameter (assuming hardware zoom capability)" |
| Extra Z / Auxiliary | 保持相机或传感器（光电二极管、高温计）对焦 |

[来源：RAYLASE SP-ICE 3 User's Manual, §7.1.1 Scan Head Format Definitions](https://www.raylase.de/en/products/electronics-control-cards/sp-ice-3.html)

同一手册定义了两个关键格式：
- `RL3_Single4D` = "XYZ plus ZoomZ"，即 **4 轴**
- `RL3_Single4DXA` = "XYZ plus ZoomZ, Extra Z, and Auxiliary"，即 **6 轴**

> ⚠️ 也就是说：RAYLASE 自己把「XY + Z + 光学变焦」叫 **4 轴（4D）**，真正的「5 轴」是 4D + 一路辅助 Z。**这是本次核查最反直觉的发现。**

手册还明确解释了 4D 与 5 轴的关系：

> "Although 4D coordinates can be sent to the card, beam magnification (in the 4th dimension) is achieved by **defocusing**. **True optical zooming would require a total of 5 axes**, but the SL protocol is limited to 2 axes per connector."
> — SP-ICE 3 User's Manual, §7.1.1 [同上]

[来源：RAYLASE SP-ICE 3 数据手册](https://www.raylase.de/en/products/electronics-control-cards/sp-ice-3.html) ✅ 另载："Controls 2-, 3-, 4- and 5-axis deflection units" 与 "Controls up to 5-axis deflection units or 2x3-axis deflection units with RL3-100 protocol"。

### 1.2 SCANLAB 的命名：只说「z 轴」，把系统叫「3D」✅

SCANLAB 不用「5 轴」这个词。其 z 轴产品页把功能描述为：

> "varioSCAN II … Adjustable focus optic; **varying of image field size, working distance and spot size**"

[来源：SCANLAB z Axes & 3D Add-Ons](https://www.scanlab.de/en/products/z-axes-3d-add-ons) ✅

RTC6 手册把系统叫 **3-axis scan system**（xy 振镜 + 1 个 z 轴动态聚焦单元）：
> "3-axis scan systems can be used for positioning the laser focus within a flat processing field without the need for a flat field objective."
[来源：SCANLAB RTC6 Manual, Ch. 8.1](https://www.scanlab.de/en/products/control-electronics/rtc-control-boards) ✅

### 1.3 Aerotech AGV5D：真正的「5 轴 = 5 个电机」，但轴名完全不同 ✅

**这是本次核查找到的最干净的「5 个电机」实例**，数据手册原文：

| 规范项 | 原文 |
|---|---|
| **Axes** | **"5 total: Spot positioning (X,Y); Focusing (Z); Precession (A,B)"** |
| Field of View | Ø 2 mm |
| Z Focus Range | 2 mm (motorized) + 4 mm (manual) |
| Positioning Resolution | 40 nm (X,Y)；30 nm (Z)；0.004 mrad (A,B) |
| Positioning Repeatability | 0.1 µm (X,Y)；0.1 µm (Z)；0.15 mrad (A,B) |
| Precession Frequency | up to 800 Hz |
| Max Precession Angle | ±9° (18° full cone) |
| Max Focusing Speed | 130 mm/s |
| Drive System | Direct-Drive Brushless Motors |
| Mass | 31 kg |

[来源：Aerotech AGV5D 数据手册 PDF](https://www.aerotech.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/agv5d-1.pdf) ✅

→ 这里的第 4、5 轴是 **A/B 进动轴（precession）**，用来控制孔锥度，**不是变焦**。

### 1.4 Scanner Optics（思特光学）的「五轴微加工系统」✅

中国厂商的产品线同时列出「四轴联动激光加工系统」与「五轴微加工钻孔系统」，五轴用途是控制孔锥度：
> "The standard 2D scan head can only be used to process the positive taper hole… Our **five-axis laser micromachining system** can not only machine standard straight holes and taper holes, but also inverted taper holes."
> 同步方式："it is necessary to process the position data of each axis under the same processor and control clock. This design uses high speed digital chip **FPGA**"

[来源：Scanner Optics 5-axis Laser Micromachining System](https://www.scanneroptics.com/products/5-axis-laser-micromachining-system/) ✅

### 1.5 ⚠️ 与「3 轴振镜 + 5 轴机械平台」的区分（用户特别要求）

这**完全是另一件事**，且厂商文档写得很清楚。Polaris Motion（PMDi）的 IFOV 页面：

> "Polaris IFOV can also coordinate a **5-axis XYZ-AC stage** with a **3D galvo scanner**."
> "3D Galvoscanners: A 3D galvo scanner can be integrated with a 5-axis CNC machine tool… Polaris Motion's 5-axis IFOV accounts for the additional **revolute axes**."

[来源：PMDi Infinite Field of View for 3-Axis and 5-Axis Machines](https://pmdi.com/capabilities/infinite-field-of-view/) ✅

→ 这里的「5 轴」= **机械平台的 X/Y/Z + A/C 两个回转轴**；振镜仍只有 XY（+Z），共 3 个电机。判定口诀：**看第 4、5 轴是电机还是伺服轴**。

### 1.6 主流命名汇总表

## 2. 完整光路：从光纤到工件

### 2.1 厂商确认的元件顺序（✅ 一手）

**RAYLASE AM-MODULE III（pre-scan / 前置聚焦架构）**

数据手册原文顺序：
1. **QBH 光纤插座** — "Laser fiber socket: QBH"（技术规范表）
2. **集成式光纤适配器 + 准直镜** — "Integrated fiber adapter and collimator for all common fiber lasers"
3. **变焦/聚焦镜组（移动镜 + 固定镜）** — "Pre-focusing beam deflection units such as the AM-MODULE III use a combination of **moving and fixed lenses in front of the scan mirrors** to focus the laser. To keep the focus in a constant z-plane, the lens is tracked and the focal length adjusted with each movement."
4. **XY 扫描振镜** — "the z-axis follows the **xy-movements of deflection units**"
5. **出射窗口 / 保护镜（第二保护玻璃）**
6. **可选：同轴过程光输出 + RAYSPECTOR 相机端口**

[来源：RAYLASE AM-MODULE III 数据手册 v3.0 (2026-03)](https://www.raylase.de/_Resources/Persistent/f/7/a/e/f7ae6ea7fe0b85465ca3d9a09ec0960c3d7aad95/2026-03-11_ds_AM-MODULE_III_EN_v3.0.pdf) ✅

**RAYLASE AXIALSCAN RD-14** 补充了 why——以及保护镜：
> "In contrast to a solution with an F-Theta lens, the laser is **focused in front of the scan mirrors**, allowing the deflection angle to be fully utilized."
> 特性列表含 "**Second protective glass** with quick-change mounting frame"
[来源：RAYLASE AXIALSCAN RD-14](https://www.raylase.de/en/products/prefocusing-deflection-units/axialscan-rd-14.html) ✅

**SCANLAB varioSCAN II（z 轴单元内部）**
> "z-axes shift the laser focus along the optical axis. This is done by the **moving optics, which expands the incoming laser beam**. Then the laser beam is either **collimated or focused by the fixed optics**."
> 结论："This results in two optics configurations: **Type FT (F-Theta)** or **Type PR (PRefocus)**… with or without an F-Theta objective."
[来源：SCANLAB varioSCAN II 数据手册 PDF（文档标注 09 / 2025）](https://www.scanlab.de/sites/default/files/2026-03/SCANLAB-varioSCANII-eBox-EN.pdf) ✅

**SCANLAB excelliSHIFT（纯反射式 z 轴）**
> "**Beam guidance: reflective**"；"the new technology uses **no transmissive optical components**. That means dispersion effects are avoided… and thermal-lens effects are minimized"
[来源：SCANLAB excelliSHIFT 数据手册 PDF](https://www.scanlab.de/sites/default/files/2025-06/SCANLAB-excelliSHIFT-EN.pdf) ✅

**准直模块（当振镜不含准直镜时）**
> "Using a Collimation Module enables direct connection of a **fiber-coupled laser** to a scan head. The typical aperture is 30 mm." 特性含 "Replaceable **protective window in front of collimation optic**"
[来源：SCANLAB Collimation Module](https://www.scanlab.de/en/products/scan-components/collimation-module) ✅

### 2.2 两条主流光路（按顺序）

**架构 A：Pre-scan（前置聚焦，AM-MODULE III / AXIALSCAN RD-14 / varioSCAN II PR）**
```
光纤激光器 ─ QBH连接器 ─ 准直镜(集成) ─ [变焦/聚焦移动镜组 + 固定镜组] ─ XY振镜 ─ 出射窗/保护镜 ─ 工件
                                          └─ 可选：同轴过程光分光 → 相机/高温计
```
特点：**无 F-θ 场镜**，靠动态聚焦做平场补偿；可充分利用振镜全偏转角 → 大幅面。

**架构 B：Post-scan（后置场镜，经典 2D + 动态聚焦）**
```
光纤 ─ QBH ─ 准直镜 ─ 扩束镜 ─ XY振镜 ─ [动态聚焦镜组] ─ F-θ 场镜 ─ 保护镜 ─ 工件
```
对应 SCANLAB varioSCAN II **Type FT**；excelliSHIFT 也按"配 f-theta 镜、f = 160 mm"标定指标。

### 2.3 哪些元件可省略 ✅

| 元件 | 可否省略 | 依据 |
|---|---|---|
| F-θ 场镜 | **可省**（用 pre-scan 动态聚焦替代） | "replace costly objectives for providing a plane focusing surface" [varioSCAN II PDF] |
| 保护镜 | **不建议省**，但形式可变 | RD-14 称 "Second protective glass"（第二片，即本身已有一片）；Collimation Module 用 "replaceable protective window" |
| 独立准直模块 | **可省**（若振镜已集成） | AM-MODULE III "integrated collimating optics" |
| 变焦镜组 | **可省** | SP-ICE 3："If optical zoom hardware is not available, it is possible to mimic, to some extent, the variation of spot diameter by **defocusing**" |
| Z 轴动态聚焦 | **可省**（退回 2D） | RTC6 中 z 坐标输出需 Option "3D" 才启用 |

## 4. 「in-focus zoom」vs 普通变焦扩束

### 4.1 专利 US 11,402,626 原文概括 ✅

专利标题 **"Deflector"**，受让人 **Raylase GmbH**，优先权日 2016-10-27，2022-08-02 授权，发明人 Carl Boehmer / Wolfgang Lehmann / Christian Schiefen / Philipp Schoen。

**核心权利要求结构**（源自摘要与说明书）：
- 一个 **第一窗口**（入光）与 **第二窗口**（出光，检测光反向进入）
- 一个 **光学元件**：**部分反射第一波长、部分透射第二波长**（即二向色分光镜）
- 一个 **XY 偏转装置**，位于第二窗口与光学元件之间
- 一个 **聚焦装置**，**可变焦距**，位于 **第一窗口与光学元件之间** → 即 **pre-scan 位置**

**变焦机理（说明书原文）**：聚焦装置含 **第一移动镜 + 第二移动镜 + 固定镜**：
- 第一移动镜（正焦距）先把光束准直；它相对第一窗口的距离同时决定**焦点位置**与**成像倍率**
- 第二移动镜（负焦距）用于**独立修正成像倍率**
- 固定镜（正焦距）在最后
- **"the deflection unit according to the invention allows any possible combination of the imaging ratio and the focal distance to be obtained within predetermined ranges by adjustment of the positions of the first movable lens and the second movable lens. Therefore the operating beam can always remain focused on the work area."**
- 且 **"the beam intensity of the operating beam, which is determined by the focal diameter, can also remain constant at different deflection angles"** → 平场补偿与恒定光斑同时成立
- 设计上保证 **在移动镜与固定镜之间没有中间焦点**（避免焦点处能量密度过高损伤镜片）

[来源：Google Patents US11402626B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US11402626B2/en) ✅ 专利分类含 **G02B15/142**（two groups only 的变焦物镜）与 **G02B7/04**（调焦/变倍镜座），佐证其为**变焦**而非单纯调焦。

### 4.2 与「普通变焦扩束」的区别 —— 厂商自己讲得最清楚 ✅

RAYLASE 应用页 "Spot Magnification in Additive Manufacturing" 原文：

| | **普通变焦扩束 / 斜入射** | **Defocus（散焦）** | **In-focus optical zoom** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 实现方式 | — | 把焦点移出材料表面 | "you need an **additional movable telescope in the beam path**, which can vary the beam diameter as an **extension for your pre-focusing deflection unit**" |
| 光斑质量 | — | "the shape of the laser spots is **poorly defined** outside the focus position"；tophat/ring mode 光斑形状丢失 | "allows you to continuously adjust the focus diameter **without leaving the focal plane**. In this way, the **original beam profile is still sharply imaged**" |
| 放大倍数 | 固定（多芯/环形光纤的 core:ring 比固定） | 连续 | 连续 1.0–2.0× |
| 原理 | — | 最小光斑直径随光束直径而变 | "the minimum spot diameter in the focus also changes with the beam diameter" |
| 限制 | 大光斑只能是环形/tophat | 功率分布需对每个放大倍率实验标定 | "the **free aperture in the beam path**, the **available space in the deflection unit** and the **power compatibility of the mirrors and optics**" |

[来源：RAYLASE — Spot Magnification in Additive Manufacturing](https://www.raylase.de/en/applications/additive-manufacturing/in-focus-spot-magnificantion-in-additive-manufacturing.html) ✅

**一句话概括**：普通变焦扩束只改光束直径、把焦点留给后级场镜；**in-focus zoom 是把"变倍望远镜"与"前置聚焦镜组"合成为一个可解耦的双移动镜系统，使"光斑直径"和"焦面位置"成为两个独立可控的自由度**——这就是为什么它需要 **2 个额外电机**，也是"5 轴"这个说法的技术根源。

同页 Figure 2/3 的 caustic 实测结论：*"magnification by means of a zoom arrangement does not significantly disturb the beam. In the case of a defocus magnification, only a shift of the focus into the material leads to reasonably defined beam shapes."*

## 5. 5 轴同时运动的控制难点 + 接口承载

### 5.1 各轴延迟不一致 —— 一手证据充分 ✅

**证据 1：RAYLASE AM-MODULE III 自己的数据就不一致**
- XY 拖曳延迟 **0.23 ms** vs 聚焦单元拖曳延迟 **1.1 ms** —— **相差近 5 倍**
- 手册提供的补救：*"When using highly dynamic silicon carbide mirrors, a small difference in dynamics still remains visible, which can be compensated using the function **Tracking Error Compensation** of the SP-ICE-3 Control Card."*
[来源：AM-MODULE III 数据手册](https://www.raylase.de/_Resources/Persistent/f/7/a/e/f7ae6ea7fe0b85465ca3d9a09ec0960c3d7aad95/2026-03-11_ds_AM-MODULE_III_EN_v3.0.pdf) ✅

**证据 2：RAYLASE 控制卡提供了逐轴补偿**
> "**Tracking Error compensation for all axes individually**"
> "Scanner Positioning Delay = Transfer Delay + Tracking Error"
[来源：SP-ICE 3 数据手册 / 用户手册 §7.3.1](https://www.raylase.de/en/products/electronics-control-cards/sp-ice-3.html) ✅

**证据 3：SCANLAB RTC6 有专门的跨轴命令 `set_timelag_compensation`**
> "Compensates the various Tracking Errors of the **xy axes and z axis**."
> 参数：`HeadNoXY, TimelagXY, TimelagZ`，单位 **[10 µs]**，范围 [0…255]
> **"Faster axes are held back towards the slower axes."**（快的等慢的）
[来源：SCANLAB RTC6 Manual, Ctrl Command set_timelag_compensation](https://www.scanlab.de/en/products/control-electronics/rtc-control-boards) ✅

→ 这条命令的存在本身，就是对"各轴延迟不一致"最权威的承认。

### 5.2 焦点补偿与光斑随变焦变化

- **Z 轴深度必须随 XY 偏转角逐点更新**（pre-scan 架构下）：*"To keep the focus in a constant z-plane, the lens is **tracked and the focal length adjusted with each movement**."* ✅（AM-MODULE III 手册）
- **变焦会改变光斑，且改变量非线性**：SCANLAB varioSCANde II 20i 的光束扩展倍率 **2.8×**，但光斑直径只放大 **√2.8 ≈ 1.67×**（20-20 FT 型，聚焦镜 f=163 mm）。[来源：varioSCAN II PDF](https://www.scanlab.de/sites/default/files/2026-03/SCANLAB-varioSCANII-eBox-EN.pdf) ✅
- **变焦会引入额外时间延迟**：RAYLASE RTC 侧对 defocus 有明确量化——
  > "execution of the directly following list command is **delayed by a few 10 µs clock cycles** due to the intermediate jump to the changed z output… but it is **at least 10 µs, even if Shift = 0**"
  [来源：RTC6 Manual, `set_defocus_list`](https://www.scanlab.de/en/products/control-electronics/rtc-control-boards) ✅
- **Z 轴需要独立的标定与校正表**：RTC6 "Checking the z axis Calibration"、`get_z_distance`、`load_z_table_no_20b`、3D 校正表（D3_*.CT5）必须专属分配给连 xy 的那个扫描头接口。✅ 同上
- **加工中飞（MOTF）时 Z 也要跟随**：RTC6 有 "**FlyZ correction**"（`set_fly_z`）对 z 轴做 Processing-on-the-fly 校正。✅ 同上

### 5.3 接口如何承载 5 个轴 —— 这是关键的工程约束 ✅

| 接口 | 轴数上限 | 帧格式/分辨率 | 承载 5 轴的方式 | 来源 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **XY2-100** | **3**（X, Y, Z） | 16-bit（标准）/ 18-bit（Enhanced）；10 µs 帧；SYNC+CLK+X+Y(+Z) 差分 | Z 走**独立数据通道**；SP-ICE 3 需 **XY2-100 Adapter Board** | ✅ [SP-ICE 3 手册](https://www.raylase.de/en/products/electronics-control-cards/sp-ice-3.html)；✅ [XY2-100 规范 PDF (Aaron Vose / Quantronix Osprey)](https://www.aaronvose.net/Quantronix_Osprey/xy2_100_specification.pdf)；✅ [sigrok XY2-100 解码器](https://sigrok.org/wiki/Protocol_decoder:Xy2-100) |
| **XY2-100-E** | 3（含 Z） | 每轴独立数据通道 + 独立回传通道 | "With XY2-100-E **each axis (x, y and also z)** of the deflection units has a data channel to the head and a data channel back to the control card." | ✅ [RAYLASE SS-III XY2-100-E 文档](https://www.raylase.de/en/products/2-axis-deflection-unit/superscan-iie.html) |
| **XY3-100** (LasIA 标准) | **5**（X, Y, Z, U, W） | 24-bit 帧载 20-bit 位置 / 32-bit 帧载 26-bit；100 kHz = 10 µs | **DB25 连接器有 X/Y/Z/U/W 五对差分线**；但 **DB15 变体只有 X/Y/Z，最多 3 轴**（"limiting the DB15-connection to a maximum of three axes"） | ✅ [LasIA Documents — XY3-100 Digital Scanner Interface v1.1 (LIA202307), ©2020-2023 by LasIA](https://sourceforge.net/p/lasia/blog/2023/07/xy3-100-digital-scanner-interface-version-11/) ✅
| **SL2-100** | **2 / 连接器** | 20-bit；SPI 帧 | "For a scanner with 3 or 4 axes, **two SL2-100 connections (and consequently two cables) are required**"；"The SL2-100 protocol has **no third channel for the Z-Axis**, so the third channel is taken instead from the **X-channel of the second SL2 interface**" → **会造成 Z 与 XY 不同步** | ✅ SP-ICE 3 手册 §7.1.1、§10.3.1 |
| **RL3-100** | **6 / 连接器** | 20-bit | "A scanner with up to 6 axes can be run on a **single data connection**. Even the most sophisticated scanner with 3D, Zoom, and a second Z axis can be operated with a **single cable**." | ✅ SP-ICE 3 手册 §7.1.1 |
| **GSBus** (Novanta) | 3 | 24-bit | "Real-time position feedback with 24-bit GSBus communication (when combined with our ScanMaster controller solution)" | ✅ [Novanta LIGHTNING II](https://novanta.com/precision-manufacturing/product/lightning-ii-3-axis-scan-head/) |

**RTC6 侧的物理约束**：3 轴系统的 xy 必须接 "Connector for First Scan Head"，z 轴接 "Connector for Second Scan Head"；且 z 坐标输出**需要启用 Option "3D"**。✅ [RTC6 Manual Ch. 8.1]

**结论**：**真正能"一根线带 5 轴"的只有 RL3-100（RAYLASE 专有）和 XY3-100（LasIA 公开标准，DB25 版）**。XY2-100 在架构上就只有 3 路，**无法原生承载 5 轴**——这直接决定了 SL2-100/XY2-100 设备做 5 轴必须用两条线，并引入跨接口同步问题。

## 6. ⚠️ 常见误解

| ❌ 流传的错误说法 | ✅ 纠正 | 依据 |
|---|---|---|
| 「5 轴振镜 = X/Y/Z/U/V 五个振镜电机」 | 业界无此统一命名。RAYLASE 把 XY+Z+ZoomZ 叫 **4D（4 轴）**，5 轴是 4D+辅助 Z；Aerotech AGV5D 的 5 轴是 XYZ+AB 进动；Scanner Optics 的 5 轴含锥度轴。**U/V 从未在振镜语境中出现**（U/W 是 XY3-100 标准的第 4/5 通道名，U/V 是激光切割机床的平板轴名）。 | ✅ SP-ICE 3 手册；✅ AGV5D |
| 「5 轴振镜 = 3 轴振镜 + 5 轴平台」 | 这是两件不同的事。厂商文档把后者写作 "5-axis **XYZ-AC stage** with a 3D galvo scanner" —— 5 个机械轴 + 3 个振镜轴。 | ✅ PMDi IFOV |
| 「变焦和 Z 轴是同一个电机」 | 在 **varioSCAN II 这类单移动镜组**产品上确实耦合（一个电机同时改焦面和光斑）；但 **in-focus zoom 架构用两个独立移动镜把两者解耦**——这正是"5 轴"存在的理由。RAYLASE 原文："**True optical zooming would require a total of 5 axes**"。 | ✅ SP-ICE 3 手册；✅ 专利 US11402626 |
| 「变焦靠离焦实现即可，不用加硬件」 | RAYLASE 明确指出离焦等效是**劣化方案**："please be aware that this approach is **inferior** to a scanner with optical zoom hardware!"；且环形/tophat 光斑在离焦下会**完全丢失形状**。 | ✅ SP-ICE 3 手册；✅ RAYLASE 应用页 |
| 「有 5 轴就一定用 5 根线/5 个连接器」 | RL3-100 单线可带 6 轴；XY3-100 DB25 单连接器带 5 轴。反之 **SL2-100 带 3 轴就要两根线**，且 Z 会与 XY 不同步。 | ✅ SP-ICE 3 手册 |
| 「5 轴振镜是 2D 振镜的简单升级，光路一样」 | 光路架构根本不同：pre-scan 架构**取消 F-θ 场镜**，把聚焦镜组放到振镜**之前**，靠动态调焦做平场补偿。 | ✅ RAYLASE AXIALSCAN RD-14 |
| 「所有 5 轴系统的第 4/5 轴用途相同」 | 完全不同的三种用途：① 光学变焦（RAYLASE ZoomZ）② 进动/锥度（Aerotech A/B、Scanner Optics）③ 辅助/传感 Z（RAYLASE Extra Z / Auxiliary，用于相机对焦）。**采购时必须问清是哪一种。** | ✅ SP-ICE 3 手册；✅ AGV5D |
